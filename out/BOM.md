## Key numbers

- AZ: Stingray-4 direct drive, 450 deg travel (1.25 turns), 15 rpm, 100 kg.cm. EL: Stingray-9 direct drive, 200 deg travel, 6.6 rpm, 227 kg.cm.
- EL axis 599 mm above the base bottom, 510 mm above the deck.
- Dish rim lowest point at EL 0/180: z = 99 mm (base top is 75 mm).
- Counterweight reach at EL 90: 388 mm; yoke plate clearance 18 mm.
