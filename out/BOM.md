## Key numbers

- AZ ratio 4.00:1 (ring 80 T / pinion 20 T, module 2). EL ratio 3.6:1 (gear 72 T / pinion 20 T).
- AZ encoder train: sun 36 T (fixed) -> idler 12 T -> sensor 36 T = exactly 1 turn per turn.
- EL axis 580 mm above the base bottom, 510 mm above the deck.
- Dish rim lowest point at EL 0/180: z = 80 mm (base top is 56 mm).
- Counterweight reach at EL 90: 388 mm; yoke plate clearance 18 mm.
